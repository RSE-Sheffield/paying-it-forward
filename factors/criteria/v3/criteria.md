Research Data

We follow the definitions and examples of research data as defined by the UKRI’s research data policy.
Definition
Research data is ‘defined as recorded information that is collected, created, generated, observed or derived from existing sources through any research method, and used to support, validate or inform research findings’.

Examples of Research Data

- creative practice process data and analysis materials
- digitised records and digital representations of physical objects,
- image, sound and video files,
- interview transcripts and survey data,
- manuscript translations,
- measurements or observations from experiments or fieldwork,
- model and simulation inputs and outputs
- software-generated outputs
- statistics
- synthetic data
- model weights

Examples of Not Research Data

- Literature and Papers
- Physical Samples
- Protocols

Research Software

We are using the current definitions of research software from the UKRI research data policy.
Definition
Research software is source code files, algorithms, scripts, computational workflows, documentation and executables that were created during the research process or for a research purpose.

Examples of Research Software
- Source code
- Packages
- Scripts
- Applications
- Model implementations
- Examples of Not Research Software
- Widely established software used for general tasks (Microsoft Excel or Google docs)
- Survey software (e.g. SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics)
- Generic statistical software (e.g. STATA)
- Generic Data processing software (e.g. Excel, Databricks, Origin)
- Generic plotting software (e.g. matplotlib)

Other Research Outputs
There are many other research outputs and artefacts that we don’t consider as data or software:

- Hardware and physical materials
- Protocols
- Instruments
- Figures
- Documentation
- Experimental designs
- Literature or reviews of literature
- Method summaries
- Survey Questions
- Proofs
- Workflows
- Interview questions
- Survey forms
- Topic guides
